# AgriNexus Financial Projections

## Revenue Assumptions
- Farmer subscriptions: R 150/month
- Cooperative subscriptions: R 2,500/month
- Marketplace commissions: 3% per transaction
- Supplier advertising: R 1,500/month per featured listing
- Premium analytics: R 500/month per enterprise account

## Year 1 Estimate
- 2,000 farmers
- 100 cooperatives
- 250 suppliers
- 1,500 monthly transactions

## Expected Revenue
Estimated annual recurring revenue at maturity is projected to grow significantly as adoption expands across agricultural clusters and public sector partnerships increase.
