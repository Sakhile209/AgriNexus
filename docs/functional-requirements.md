# AgriNexus Functional Requirements

## Core Functionalities
1. User registration and authentication
2. Role-based access for farmer, cooperative, government, supplier, buyer, and consumer
3. Farm and livestock management
4. Crop planning and activity logging
5. Vaccination and animal health monitoring
6. Weather alerts and market price updates
7. Product catalog and marketplace ordering
8. Notification and alert system
9. Dashboard and reporting modules
10. AI assistant advisory features

## Business Workflow Requirements
- Farmers can create and manage farms and livestock records.
- Buyers can search products and place orders.
- Suppliers can publish listings and track performance.
- Government users can view aggregate insights and outbreak trends.
- Cooperatives can manage member records and reports.
