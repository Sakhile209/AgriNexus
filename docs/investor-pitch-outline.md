# AgriNexus Investor Pitch Outline

## 1. Problem
Smallholder and commercial farmers lack connected tools for farm management, animal health, weather intelligence, and market access.

## 2. Solution
AgriNexus is an integrated digital agriculture platform that improves productivity, reduces losses, and connects farmers to markets and public services.

## 3. Market Opportunity
Africa's agriculture sector is large, fragmented, and under-digitized. There is strong demand for accessible digital tools that improve efficiency and income.

## 4. Business Model
Subscription revenue, marketplace commissions, supplier advertising, government partnerships, and premium analytics.

## 5. Traction Strategy
Pilot with cooperatives, expand through case studies, establish public-private partnerships, and build a growing network of suppliers and buyers.

## 6. Growth Potential
Nationwide rollout, regional expansion, and value-added services such as insurance, advisory, and logistics integrations.

## 7. Ask
Funding for product development, market expansion, partnerships, and initial growth operations.
