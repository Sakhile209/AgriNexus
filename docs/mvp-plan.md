# AgriNexus MVP Plan

## MVP Scope
The MVP will focus on the core needs of farmers and buyers while proving the marketplace and advisory value.

## Included in MVP
- Farmer onboarding and profile management
- Farm registration
- Livestock and crop record tracking
- Basic vaccination reminders
- Marketplace listing and order creation
- Dashboard with key KPIs
- Mobile-friendly experience

## Out of Scope for MVP
- Full government analytics suite
- Advanced AI diagnostics across all crop types
- Cross-border multi-country deployment
- Large-scale supplier campaign automation
