# AgriNexus Marketing Strategy

## Brand Positioning
AgriNexus will position itself as a practical digital farming platform that helps growers improve productivity, lower losses, and access better markets.

## Core Channels
- Cooperative partnerships
- Agricultural extension programs
- Digital advertising and social media campaigns
- Farmer workshops and field demonstrations
- Referral and ambassador programs

## Messaging
- Smarter farming decisions
- Better livestock care
- More transparent market access
- Stronger food systems

## Early Adoption Strategy
Focus on pilot regions with strong farming density, cooperative networks, and linkages to buyers and government support programs.
