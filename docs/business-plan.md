# AgriNexus Business Plan

## Executive Summary
AgriNexus is a digital agriculture platform designed to improve productivity, reduce livestock losses, and strengthen market access for farmers across South Africa and later other African markets. The platform consolidates farm management, livestock monitoring, weather intelligence, disease tracking, marketplace access, and analytics into one connected ecosystem.

## Value Proposition
- Digitize farm records and advisory workflows
- Reduce disease and livestock mortality through reminders and health tracking
- Improve farm decision-making through weather and market intelligence
- Build trusted buyer-supplier-farmer connections through a digital marketplace

## Target Customers
- Smallholder and commercial farmers
- Agricultural cooperatives
- Government departments and extension services
- Suppliers of seed, fertilizer, feed, and equipment
- Buyers and consumers seeking transparent sourcing

## Revenue Model
- Subscription plans for farmers and cooperatives
- Marketplace commissions on completed transactions
- Supplier advertising and featured listings
- Government contracts for extension and reporting tools
- Premium analytics and forecasting services

## Go-to-Market Strategy
1. Pilot with cooperatives and farmer groups in high-activity agricultural regions.
2. Offer low-friction onboarding with mobile-first field usage.
3. Create value through free basic accounts and paid premium insights.
4. Expand through partnerships with agribusinesses, NGOs, and public agencies.

## Growth Strategy
AgriNexus will scale from a South African pilot to regional expansion through modular deployment, multilingual support, and localized advisory content.
